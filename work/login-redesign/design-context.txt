# PRODUCT.md

# Koonpinai

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Radix dialogs and Lucide React. `next-themes` provides appearance preferences. Auth.js's official Keycloak provider connects the approved KMITL OIDC client; Drizzle/PostgreSQL persists accounts and revocable application-session records. Typed fictional data supplies guarded directory reads; PDF.js, Tesseract.js and Zod provide local document processing. No timetable publishing or university timetable API.

## Users
Approximately 50 university friends, primarily KMITL students, checking who has class and when they can meet. Shared access requires a completed profile, current policy acknowledgement and administrator approval. Administrators manage pending/approved/suspended accounts.

## Product Purpose
Help friends find a break together: show who is free, how long their free window lasts, and what comes next without messaging everyone individually.

## Positioning
Availability is calculated from recurring academic timetables in Asia/Bangkok. It describes a schedule, not attendance or physical location.

## Operating Context
Quick mobile checks between classes; weekly planning on larger screens. The approved Contemporary Social Utility (Concept C) interface leads with free-window rows, then a compact in-class roster. Twelve existing fictional people remain the public data set. English interface copy, Thai branding and Thai names are confirmed.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The directory and friend schedules require an approved account with a current policy acknowledgement and completed profile. Search nicknames, filter availability, inspect course/room/next-class information and open timetable details. Preserve exact class boundaries, semester dates, empty days and overlapping sessions. Records remain clearly fictional until a separately authorized timetable storage flow exists.

The real KMITL PDF importer uses positioned PDF.js text or local Thai/English OCR, editable source comparison, Zod validation, explicit review confirmation, private timetable/profile preview and canonical JSON export. Personal import reuses that workflow and can apply an accepted timetable to the in-memory My Timetable preview. Source PDFs keep their original colors in both themes. Original files, extracted text and imported schedules are not uploaded, logged or stored in browser persistence by the application.

Profile setup, versioned policy acknowledgement, account approval/suspension and profile settings persist in PostgreSQL. Identity is the verified OIDC issuer/subject pair, never a nickname or email link. Auth.js encrypted JWT cookies are backed by active, expiring/revocable database session records. Local logout does not terminate the university SSO session. Personal imported timetables and admin friend/schedule edits remain temporary and never publish; appearance persists locally under `koonpinai-theme`.

The explicit `ENABLE_UI_PREVIEW=true` fictional mode is available only in development with all real client/session/database credentials absent. Partial real configuration disables it; production never allows it. `ENABLE_ADMIN_DEMO` no longer grants access. `/login` starts real KMITL sign-in when fully configured. Private routes fail closed; server reads/actions recheck identity, policy, approval and admin role independently of the proxy. Private PDFs, credentials and derived inspection files stay out of Git.

## Brand Commitments
Koonpinai / คุณไปไหน. “Find a break together.” Preserve the geometric K wordmark, bilingual names, rounded-square initials, ruled directory groups and free-window-first hierarchy. The user-selected shadcn preset `b3FNbq6SRM` now supplies the visual foundation: Nova/Radix, zinc neutrals, sky actions, blue chart tokens, Lucide icons, Inter body, JetBrains Mono headings and medium rounding. Noto Sans Thai provides Thai coverage. Light and dark appearances share content and geometry with readable text and input boundaries. Green availability and amber class states remain semantic; six course identities remain consistent across themes. The earlier selected sibling prototype remains composition evidence, not current palette or typography authority, product records or a replacement schedule engine.

## Evidence on Hand
The current design foundation was decoded and applied with the official shadcn CLI using preset `b3FNbq6SRM`. Canonical preset variables and shared/importer/admin styles live in `src/app/globals.css`; product semantic extensions and effective geometry live in `src/app/redesign.css`, layered after the inherited composition in `src/app/approved-design.css`. `DESIGN.md` and `.impeccable/design.json` record the current system. The historical Contemporary Social Utility prototype and desktop/mobile PNGs remain at `D:/Project/Koonpinai-design-previews/design-previews/selected/`.

Public records are fictional; PDF fixtures are sanitized and generated in memory. A user-supplied two-page scanned KMITL PDF was tested locally without committing the original or private derived evidence; its detailed table yielded nine sessions. Automated suites cover schedule boundaries, parsing, real local OCR, review/export, theme tokens and browser behavior. Test commands and scope are in README; final verification is reported separately rather than assumed by this document.

## Product Principles
- A glance should answer who is free and for how long.
- Timetable truth, deliberate review and explicit preview boundaries come first.
- Preserve mobile readability, keyboard operation and maintainable components.
- Theme preference must not turn private schedule data into persistent browser data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Visible keyboard focus, written availability labels, labeled native controls, Radix dialog interaction, skip navigation and reduced-motion support. Aim for normal text contrast of at least 4.5:1, large text 3:1, and discernible input/focus boundaries. Header theme targets are 44px. Phones use single-column free rows, current/next-class roster lines and fixed bottom navigation; weekly grids become a day agenda instead of shrinking seven columns.

## Open Decisions
Public KMITL discovery, local client configuration and PostgreSQL migration/connectivity were verified. The user encountered a 502 on the university SSO host during real browser sign-in, so a successful real callback/token exchange remains unverified. Complete that approved-client test and production callback/HTTPS checks before launch. RP-Initiated Logout requires confirmed client post-logout registration/token-hint handling. Policy/privacy/removal rules, backups, rate limits and session cleanup need operational review. Timetable persistence, GitHub JSON storage, the university timetable API and deployment remain out of scope. See AUTH.md for configuration and exact test boundaries.

# Design System: Koonpinai

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Find a break together"**

Koonpinai makes a free window easy to spot. The user-selected shadcn preset `b3FNbq6SRM` supplies the Nova/Radix foundation: zinc neutrals, sky actions, blue chart tokens, Lucide icons, Inter body text, JetBrains Mono headings and medium rounding. The geometric K wordmark, bilingual names, rounded-square initials and ruled schedule groups preserve the product identity.

The working surface stays compact and left aligned. Time and free-window duration provide the visual signature; green and amber describe availability while stable course hues identify subjects. Light and dark appearances share content, geometry and hierarchy. Restrained transitions explain changes to people and filters, with reduced-motion preferences respected.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Free-window-first hierarchy with monospaced time.
- Zinc surfaces, sky actions and stable semantic schedule colors.
- Inter and JetBrains Mono with Noto Sans Thai coverage.
- Flat grouped rows and medium rounded controls.
- Equivalent Light, Dark and System appearance with keyboard and reduced-motion support.

## Colors

The palette combines quiet zinc surfaces with sky actions and retained schedule semantics. Frontmatter records effective reused light tokens and their changed `dark-` counterparts. CSS custom properties remain the implementation source: canonical preset tokens live in `src/app/globals.css`; product extensions live in `src/app/redesign.css`, loaded after `src/app/approved-design.css`.

### Primary

- **Sky action:** `primary` and `primary-foreground` are the preset button pair. `link` and `focus` provide readable links, active navigation, the K mark and keyboard outlines; their dark values are lighter than the button fill. `primary-hover` gives filled buttons their hover state.
- **Availability green / class amber:** `free` and `class` pairs describe schedule availability. Free-window rows put duration in green and the written status beneath it without a badge background.
- **Feedback red:** the danger pairs identify destructive actions and feedback; warning borders and washes pair with explicit review text.

### Secondary

Six retained subject pairs (`course-0` through `course-5`) identify courses across both themes. Four avatar pairs add restrained initials variety without encoding availability. The preset's blue chart scale remains available in the canonical stylesheet; it does not replace schedule subject identity.

### Neutral

`app-canvas` is the effective page canvas, distinct from the canonical shadcn `background` token. `card` is the content surface; `foreground` is main ink and `muted-foreground` is supporting text. `muted` remains a surface token and must not be used for text. Quiet `border` rules group content; `input-border` gives controls a stronger visible edge. `today`, `selection`, `status-neutral`, `overlay` and ribbon roles supply contextual treatments. Source PDFs preserve their original colors in either appearance.

**The Status Has Words Rule.** Availability always includes a written label; color reinforces rather than replaces its meaning.

**The Course Identity Rule.** Keep the six course identities stable across themes and separate from availability meaning. Preserve source-document colors.

## Typography

**Display / Heading Font:** JetBrains Mono through `next/font/google`, with Noto Sans Thai Variable and monospace fallback.
**Body Font:** Inter through `next/font/google`, with Noto Sans Thai Variable from `@fontsource-variable/noto-sans-thai` and sans-serif fallback.

Headings and key schedule times share a precise monospace texture. Names, descriptions, forms and navigation use Inter. Existing typography weights vary locally; shared buttons retain (650), main headings use (600), and subheadings retain (700).

### Hierarchy

- **Display:** login story uses `display`; it is a route-specific scale rather than a global hero pattern.
- **Headline:** standard desktop page headings use `headline`; import/onboarding/admin headings use (34px). Mobile page headings use `headline-mobile`, with the narrow directory heading at (26px).
- **Title:** generic section headings use `title`; directory headings use (19px), then (18px) on mobile. The schedule summary uses (23px), then (21px) on mobile.
- **Body:** global `body`; explanatory paragraphs retain a maximum of (64ch). Page introductions use (14px), then (13px) on mobile.
- **Label / status:** shared buttons use `label`, status badges use `status`, and timetable metadata keeps local compact sizes.
- **Free window:** the main duration uses `free-window`, then (16px) on mobile and (14px) at the narrowest breakpoint. The Bangkok clock uses (24px), then (17px) on mobile.

Text-entry fields use (16px) on mobile. Times, schedule axes and time inputs retain tabular numerals.

**The Time Leads Rule.** Use monospaced, tabular time for the main clock and free-window duration; keep names and explanatory copy in the body family.

**The Bilingual Reading Rule.** Retain Noto Sans Thai coverage wherever the Latin families cannot render Thai text.

Search, full-width filters and view controls wrap without discarding content.

## Elevation & Depth

Ordinary directory rows and panels remain flat, separated by tone and rules. Hover changes tone without lifting rows. The selected availability segment uses a subtle (0 1px 3px #00000012) shadow. Dialogs and the theme popover use theme-specific `dialog-shadow` values recorded in the sidecar.

**The Flat Group Rule.** Use tone, spacing and rules for ordinary hierarchy. Keep stronger cast shadows on dialogs and the theme popover; a subtle selected-filter shadow is an intentional exception.

Shared state changes use (160ms ease). Dialogs arrive over (180ms ease-out), the theme menu over (140ms ease-out), and native React view transitions explain shared person identity and directory updates over (220ms) with (cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)). Header and bottom navigation stay visually stable during transitions. Reduced-motion preferences remove CSS animations/transitions and gate React view-transition participation. Skeletons remain loading feedback rather than page choreography.

## Shapes

The preset base radius is (0.625rem). Its reused derived scale is recorded in `rounded`: controls and initials use `control`, selected segments use `md`, and directory/dialog/summary containers use `container`. Status badges retain their compact `badge` shape; timetable blocks retain (7px) rounding. This is a coherent shared scale with purposeful legacy component exceptions, not a requirement to flatten every geometry to one radius.

Boundaries are (1px). Keyboard controls generally use a (3px) sky outline offset by (3px); shared fields use a (2px) focus outline offset by (3px). Whole-row links inset the outline to keep it visible within clipped groups. Theme switch/disclosure and dialog-close targets are (44px). Initials stay rounded squares and retain their existing responsive sizes.

## Components

### Buttons

Primary actions use the preset sky pair; default shared buttons are at least (44px) high with (9px 16px) padding and `control` rounding. Outline actions use a card surface, link ink and the stronger input edge; ghost actions use transparent fill. Both hover to a surface tint. Destructive actions retain semantic danger ink on a danger wash and change ink/border on hover. Pressed buttons move down (1px). Disabled controls use opacity (0.45) and a not-allowed cursor. SSO keeps label-first order and its existing configured/disabled behavior.

### Inputs / Fields

Shared fields use a card surface, foreground ink, input edge, (45px) minimum height and (9px 12px) padding. Mobile fields retain (47px) minimum height and (16px) text. Search embeds a real text input and outline icon with a focus-within treatment. Invalid fields use danger borders; uncertain import values retain warning borders and written review messages. Native labels, required/disabled semantics and focus feedback remain intact.

### Navigation

Desktop links combine Lucide outline icons and medium text, (10px 14px) padding and `control` rounding. Active links use `link` ink on the `today` wash; mobile bottom navigation follows the same distinction. Theme controls retain a direct Sun/Moon switch and Appearance disclosure with Light/Dark/System radios, Escape dismissal and focus return. System appearance is the default and only the visual preference persists locally.

### Chips

Status badges pair a dot with written labels: free/upcoming green, in-class amber and no-schedule/out-of-term neutral. Free rows display that status unboxed beneath duration. Availability filters show names and counts, using a soft surface tray; selected filters use card fill, foreground text, `md` rounding and the subtle selected shadow. Day selection retains the primary pair.

### Cards / Containers

The directory is a flat grouped container with ruled child links. Free rows put identity left and free-window duration right; in-class rows preserve course, room and next-class content. Panels, summaries and policy/access containers use the same card surface and restrained boundaries. Keep real typed records and the existing whole-row profile links.

### Timetable and Agenda

Six subject pairs color events consistently across themes. The current class has an amber inset marker and today has a contextual wash. Preserve exact duration, position and overlap columns. The mobile agenda exposes start/end time, course and room as readable content. Short events may hide secondary metadata instead of distorting duration. Events open the existing Radix detail dialog.

### Dialogs, PDF Review and Account Surfaces

Dialogs use `container` rounding, theme-aware shadow, a (44px) close target and Radix keyboard/focus behavior. Import source previews keep actual PDF colors, page/zoom controls and a keyboard-focusable source region. Review retains individual sessions, source references, editable fields, warnings and explicit confirmation. Mobile panel switches share the same content model.

Account, policy, approval and access surfaces inherit this vocabulary. Preserve authenticated identity, server-validated status, pending/success/error feedback, explicit fictional notices and temporary-import boundaries. Visual changes never imply timetable persistence or publishing.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** use the user-selected preset as the foundation and keep product extensions distinct from canonical preset tokens.
- **Do** retain the K wordmark, bilingual names, free-window hierarchy and monospaced time.
- **Do** keep course identities consistent across themes and show PDF source colors faithfully.
- **Do** retain written statuses, visible focus, labeled controls and reduced-motion behavior.
- **Do** preserve readable mobile current/next-class lines and a day agenda.
- **Do** label fictional data, temporary edits and disconnected account actions honestly.

### Don't:

- **Don't** restore the superseded periwinkle/Manrope visual authority.
- **Don't** use course colors as availability meaning.
- **Don't** add heavy decorative shadows, gradients or neon accents.
- **Don't** treat compact timetable metadata sizes as the default for new body text.
- **Don't** imply that preview edits save, publish, submit approval or grant access.
