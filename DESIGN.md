# Design System: Koonpinai

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Cloud & Sky"**

Koonpinai uses the user-approved Cloud & Sky system across login, the directory, timetables, imports, account setup, policy, approval and administration. The pastel-blue peeking penguin is the sole brand mark, paired with the exact live-text name Koonpinai. Mali headings and Kodchasan UI give English and Thai a soft, readable character; light rules, restrained curves and gentle elevation keep useful controls and data prominent.

The existing shadcn Nova/Radix foundation remains, while the V3 palette, typography and mascot supersede all earlier visual directions. Light mode uses a pale-blue canvas, white surfaces and navy ink; dark/system mode uses derived navy surfaces. Login deliberately keeps the light palette under either OS theme. Missing data remains an honest empty state; development samples and temporary imports never imply a real shared-timetable service.

**Key Characteristics:**

- One approved peeking penguin and the exact Koonpinai name.
- Cloud & Sky across every route, with a derived navy dark appearance.
- Mali headings and Kodchasan body/UI, loaded once with Thai and Latin coverage.
- Compact schedule workspaces, readable mobile rows and useful empty states.
- Written status, visible focus, deliberate review and reduced-motion support.

### Primary

- **Brand blue:** `brand-primary` identifies the brand accent and light focus treatment. Keep it distinct from the accessible interactive surface.
- **Action blue:** `primary` with `primary-foreground` gives white button text (5.26:1) contrast; `primary-hover` darkens it. `link` and `focus` adapt for readability on dark surfaces.
- **Availability green / class amber:** the `free` and `class` pairs retain useful schedule meaning. The mascot's small warm beak belongs to the approved artwork, not an added UI accent.
- **Feedback red:** danger pairs support destructive actions and feedback. Warning boundaries and washes always accompany explicit review text.

### Secondary

Six retained course pairs distinguish subjects in either theme; they do not describe availability. Avatar backgrounds use quiet blue variations with readable blue ink. The existing blue chart scale remains in the foundation without replacing course identity.

### Neutral

The canvas is `background`, with `app-canvas` resolving to it. `card` supplies content surfaces, `foreground` main ink, and `muted-foreground` supporting text. `muted` remains a surface role. Quiet `border` rules group content; `input-border` makes controls discernible. Dark mode derives navy canvas/card/surface roles with light ink and brighter links. Source PDFs retain original colors rather than inheriting the UI palette.

**The Single Identity Rule.** Use the approved peeking penguin and exact Koonpinai wordmark everywhere; never substitute the former K symbol or translate the official name.

**The Status Has Words Rule.** Availability always includes a written label; color reinforces rather than replaces its meaning.

**The Course Identity Rule.** Keep the six course identities stable across themes and separate from availability meaning. Preserve source-document colors.
